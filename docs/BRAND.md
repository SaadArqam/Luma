# Brand Guidelines

This document defines the Luma brand identity, including visual identity, voice, and usage guidelines.

## Brand Identity

### Brand Name

**Luma** - Derived from "illumination" and "light," representing clarity, understanding, and insight. The name reflects the product's purpose: helping users see their life clearly.

### Tagline

"Understand your life."

### Brand Essence

Luma is a calm, intelligent companion that helps users understand their life through thoughtful design and AI-powered insights.

### Brand Personality

- **Calm:** Peaceful, composed, never frantic
- **Intelligent:** Smart, thoughtful, never showy
- **Warm:** Friendly, approachable, never cold
- **Premium:** Refined, polished, never flashy
- **Trustworthy:** Reliable, secure, never risky

### Brand Attributes

- Calm premium minimalism
- AI-powered but AI-invisible
- Holistic life understanding
- Privacy-first
- User-centric design

## Visual Identity

### Logo

**Logo Concept:** A simple, elegant mark representing illumination and clarity.

**Logo Usage:**
- Use the logo on all marketing materials
- Maintain clear space around the logo
- Never stretch or distort the logo
- Use the appropriate logo variant for the background

### Color Palette

Luma uses a warm, neutral color palette with a single accent color.

**Primary Colors:**
- Background: Warm off-white (not pure white)
- Surface: Slightly darker warm tones
- Text: Dark warm ink (not pure black)
- Accent: Subtle accent color for primary actions

**Semantic Colors:**
- Success: Muted green
- Warning: Muted amber
- Error: Muted red

**Dark Mode:**
- Background: Soft charcoal (not pure black)
- Surface: Darker warm tones
- Text: Warm white
- Accent: Same accent color adjusted for dark mode

*For specific color values, refer to `.claude/DESIGN-luma.md`*

### Typography

**Primary Typeface:** Geist Sans (system-ui fallback)
- Display: Large, confident headlines with tight tracking
- Body: Clean, readable body text
- Financial Values: Geist Mono for numbers

**Typography Principles:**
- Limited font weights (400, 500, 600)
- Tight tracking on display sizes
- Comfortable line height for readability
- Monospace for financial values creates visual focal points

*For specific typography values, refer to `.claude/DESIGN-luma.md`*

### Spacing

**Base Unit:** 8px

**Spacing Philosophy:** Generous whitespace creates calm. Never fill space just because it exists.

**Spacing Scale:** 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 80, 96px

*For specific spacing values, refer to `.claude/DESIGN-luma.md`*

### Shape Language

**Border Radius:** Rounded corners create a friendly, approachable feel.

**Radius Scale:**
- Small: 8px
- Medium: 12px
- Large: 16px
- Full: 9999px (for pills and circles)

**Shape Philosophy:** Rounded corners feel friendlier and more approachable than sharp corners.

### Motion

**Motion Philosophy:** Motion is functional, never decorative. It explains state changes and confirms actions.

**Motion Principles:**
- Fast transitions (150-200ms)
- Natural easing curves
- Subtle, purposeful animations
- Respect reduced motion preferences

*For specific motion values, refer to `.claude/DESIGN-luma.md`*

### Photography Style

**Photography Philosophy:** If photography is used, it should feel calm, warm, and authentic.

**Guidelines:**
- Warm, natural lighting
- Authentic, not staged
- People-focused, not product-focused
- Calm compositions
- Avoid cluttered backgrounds

### Illustration Style

**Illustration Philosophy:** Illustrations should be simple, clean, and supportive of content.

**Guidelines:**
- Minimal line art
- Limited color palette
- Support content, don't distract
- Simple shapes and forms
- Avoid decorative elements

## Brand Voice

### Voice Characteristics

Luma's voice is:

- **Warm:** Friendly and approachable
- **Confident:** Knowledgeable but not arrogant
- **Clear:** Simple and direct
- **Respectful:** Never talks down to users
- **Calm:** Never urgent or frantic

### Writing Style

**Principles:**
- Use simple, clear language
- Avoid jargon and technical terms
- Be concise and direct
- Use active voice
- Write for the user, not about the product

**Examples:**

**Instead of:**
- "Luma leverages advanced machine learning algorithms to provide predictive insights."
- "Our proprietary context engine utilizes sophisticated relevance signals."

**Use:**
- "Luma helps you see patterns in your spending."
- "We show you what matters most right now."

### Tone Guidelines

**Do:**
- Use "you" to address the user
- Be conversational but professional
- Explain complex concepts simply
- Acknowledge user emotions
- Be honest about limitations

**Don't:**
- Use corporate speak or buzzwords
- Talk down to users
- Overpromise or exaggerate
- Use technical jargon unnecessarily
- Be overly casual or informal

## Brand Applications

### Website

**Header:** Simple, clean navigation with logo

**Hero:** Clear value proposition, calm imagery, single CTA

**Body:** Generous whitespace, clear hierarchy, focused content

**Footer:** Simple links, legal information, social icons

### Marketing Materials

**Principles:**
- Focus on user benefits, not features
- Use calm, warm imagery
- Clear, concise copy
- Single primary call-to-action
- Generous whitespace

### Social Media

**Voice:** Conversational, helpful, authentic

**Content:**
- Tips and insights
- User stories
- Product updates
- Behind-the-scenes
- Educational content

**Guidelines:**
- Engage with followers
- Respond to comments
- Share user-generated content
- Avoid overly promotional content
- Maintain consistent posting schedule

### Email Communications

**Subject Lines:** Clear, concise, benefit-focused

**Body:**
- Personal and conversational
- Clear value proposition
- Single call-to-action
- Mobile-friendly design
- Easy unsubscribe

### App Interface

**Principles:**
- Calm, minimal design
- Clear information hierarchy
- Generous whitespace
- Intuitive navigation
- Consistent branding elements

## Brand Dos and Don'ts

### Do

- Use the logo consistently
- Maintain generous whitespace
- Use the approved color palette
- Follow typography guidelines
- Keep copy clear and simple
- Focus on user benefits
- Maintain calm, premium aesthetic
- Respect user intelligence
- Be honest and transparent
- Use motion purposefully

### Don't

- Stretch or distort the logo
- Use unapproved colors
- Use unauthorized fonts
- Crowd the design
- Use jargon or technical terms
- Overpromise or exaggerate
- Use urgent or frantic language
- Talk down to users
- Hide important information
- Use decorative motion

## Brand Consistency

### Why Consistency Matters

Consistent branding builds:
- Trust and credibility
- Brand recognition
- User loyalty
- Professional image
- Clear communication

### Maintaining Consistency

- Use approved brand assets
- Follow these guidelines
- Review materials against brand standards
- Train team members on brand guidelines
- Audit marketing materials regularly
- Update guidelines as needed

### Brand Assets

**Available Assets:**
- Logo files (SVG, PNG)
- Color swatches
- Typography samples
- Templates (email, social media)
- Brand guidelines document

**Asset Management:**
- Store assets in central location
- Use version control
- Provide access to team members
- Update assets when guidelines change
- Archive outdated assets

## Brand Evolution

### Principles for Evolution

The Luma brand may evolve over time, but:

- Core brand essence remains constant
- Changes should be gradual, not abrupt
- User feedback informs evolution
- Maintain brand recognition
- Document changes clearly

### When to Evolve

- New product features require brand updates
- User feedback suggests improvements
- Market conditions change
- Competitive landscape shifts
- Technology advances

### Evolution Process

1. Identify need for change
2. Research and gather feedback
3. Develop concepts
4. Test with users
5. Refine based on feedback
6. Implement changes
7. Update brand guidelines
8. Communicate changes to team

## Brand Protection

### Trademark Usage

- Use the ® symbol when appropriate
- Don't use the logo in misleading ways
- Don't modify the logo
- Don't use the logo to endorse other products
- Follow trademark laws in your region

### Copyright

- All brand materials are copyrighted
- Don't reproduce without permission
- Credit Luma when using brand materials
- Follow fair use guidelines

### Brand Misuse

If you encounter brand misuse:
- Document the misuse
- Contact the brand owner
- Request correction or removal
- Take legal action if necessary
- Educate the misuser about guidelines
